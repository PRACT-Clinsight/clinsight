Connecting Deep Molecular Science, Regulatory Standards, and Clinical Evidence

Ecosystem Architecture

The Three Core PRACT Intelligence Modules

Each module provides specialized, open exploration tools focused on a distinct pillar of translational therapeutics:

ClinSight™ | Clinical Trials

An open explorer for pivotal Phase 3 clinical trials across Oncology, ADCs, CAR-T, and Incretin mimetics. Structured protocols, primary endpoints, and direct links to ClinicalTrials.gov.

  • 120+ Pivotal Studies Indexed
  • Filter by Phase, Drug Modality & Year
  • Primary Registry Links & PubMed Citations
Launch clinsight.pract.eu

RegSight™ | Regulatory Guidance

A structured reference catalog tracking global statutory guidance documents across FDA, EMA, and MDCG. Designed to demystify complex therapeutic submission standards.

  • Global FDA & EMA Guidance Standards
  • Medical Device & Pharma Guidelines
  • Structured Statutory Search
Explore regsight.pract.eu

VigiSight™ | Pharmacovigilance

Post-market safety signal analysis exploring real-world adverse event data through open FAERS reports and published pharmacovigilance literature.

  • FAERS Spontaneous Reporting Insights
  • Targeted Toxicity Signals (ADCs, TKIs)
  • Benefit-Risk Modeling
Explore vigisight.pract.eu
