Patient Eligibility Checklist

Key Inclusion Criteria

  • Histologically confirmed diagnosis for Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus, Obesity, Functional Hypogonadism
  • Measurable disease according to standardized response evaluation criteria
  • Adequate baseline organ and bone marrow function
  • Age ≥ 18 years at time of written informed consent

Key Exclusion Criteria

  • Active, clinically uncontrolled central nervous system (CNS) metastases
  • Severe or uncontrolled concurrent systemic disease or active infection
  • Prior intolerance or hypersensitivity to study intervention
  • Participation in another investigational intervention within protocol window
