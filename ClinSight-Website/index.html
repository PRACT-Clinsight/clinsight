Open Science Principles

Data Provenance & Methodology

Every record in ClinSight is ingested directly from the official ClinicalTrials.gov REST API (National Library of Medicine / NIH). We prioritize pivotal Phase 3 trials because they form the statutory foundation for global regulatory decisions and clinical practice guidelines.

We believe clinical evidence should be completely accessible without commercial paywalls, opaque rating algorithms, or sales pitches. Each study page provides direct one-click links to the verbatim government registry record and open medical literature search engines.
