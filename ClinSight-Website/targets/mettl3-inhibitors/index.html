METTL3 RNA Methyltransferase Inhibitors — Clinical Trial Landscape

Side-by-side comparison of all clinical-stage interventional trials evaluating oral METTL3 inhibitors (STC-15 and EP102) across dose-escalation designs, DLT windows, combination regimens, and patient eligibility criteria.

Executive Class Synopsis (Manuscript / IB Background Ready)

Two small-molecule inhibitors targeting the m6A RNA methyltransferase METTL3 have advanced into human oncology trials: STORM Therapeutics' STC-15 and Epics Therapeutics' EP102. STC-15 completed its 4-cohort first-in-human Phase 1 monotherapy dose-escalation trial in advanced solid tumors (NCT05584111; 3+3 design with modified Fibonacci increments across 6 US centers; PMID: 40751015) and is now in an international Phase 1b/2 combination trial with the anti-PD-1 antibody toripalimab (NCT06975293; 3+3 dose escalation followed by Simon's two-stage expansion in NSCLC, HNSCC, melanoma, and endometrial cancer across 21 sites in the US, UK, and Australia). Epics Therapeutics' EP102 is recruiting a first-in-human Phase 1/2 trial (NCT07163325 across 6 sites in Belgium, France, and Spain) utilizing a Bayesian Optimal Interval (BOIN) dose-escalation design with a 21-day DLT assessment window, evaluating EP102 both as monotherapy in solid and myeloid malignancies and in combination cohorts with pembrolizumab or azacitidine plus venetoclax.

NCT06975293 PHASE1/PHASE2 RECRUITING

STC-15 as a Part of Combination Therapy With Toripalimab in Selected Advanced Cancers and as Monotherapy in Participants With Selected Sarcomas

Sponsor: STORM Therapeutics LTD · Regimen: Oral STC-15 + Toripalimab (anti-PD-1)
View Trial Synopsis & Details →

NCT06975293 (STORM Therapeutics) is a recruiting Phase 1b/2 multicenter study evaluating the oral METTL3 inhibitor STC-15 in combination with the anti-PD-1 antibody toripalimab across 21 sites in the United States, United Kingdom, and Australia. The Phase 1b portion uses a 3+3 dose-escalation design to evaluate safety, tolerability, and dose-limiting toxicities (DLTs), followed by a Simon's two-stage Phase 2 expansion across four disease-specific cohorts: non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC), head and neck squamous cell carcinoma (HNSCC), melanoma, and endometrial cancer.

NCT07163325 PHASE1 RECRUITING

EP102 Safety and Efficacy in METTL3 Modulation in Advanced Solid Tumors

Sponsor: Epics Therapeutics · Regimen: Oral EP102 monotherapy & combinations (+ pembrolizumab or + azacitidine/venetoclax)
View Trial Synopsis & Details →

NCT07163325 (Epics Therapeutics) is a recruiting Phase 1/2 first-in-human study of the oral METTL3 inhibitor EP102 in patients with advanced solid tumors and hematologic malignancies across 6 clinical sites in Belgium, France, and Spain. Dose escalation follows a Bayesian Optimal Interval (BOIN) design with a 21-day DLT assessment window after first administration to determine the maximum tolerated dose (MTD) and recommended expansion doses, evaluating EP102 both as monotherapy and in combination with pembrolizumab or azacitidine plus venetoclax.

NCT05584111 PHASE1 COMPLETED

Oral Administration of STC-15 in Subjects With Advanced Malignancies

Sponsor: STORM Therapeutics LTD · Regimen: Oral STC-15 monotherapy (4 dose-escalation cohorts)
View Trial Synopsis & Details →

NCT05584111 (STORM Therapeutics) is the completed Phase 1 first-in-human (FIH) monotherapy study of the oral METTL3 RNA methyltransferase inhibitor STC-15 in patients with advanced solid malignancies across 6 United States cancer centers. The trial enrolled 4 sequential dose-escalation cohorts using a 3+3 design with modified Fibonacci dose increments to evaluate safety, pharmacokinetics, target engagement, and the Recommended Phase 2 Dose (RP2D), with peer-reviewed clinical results published in 2025 (PMID: 40751015).

Note on non-interventional registry records: A fourth ClinicalTrials.gov record mentioning METTL3 (NCT06762925, Xijing Hospital) is an observational biomarker tissue study with no drug intervention and is therefore excluded from this interventional drug comparison.
