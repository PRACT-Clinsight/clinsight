🛡️ Corrections Policy

FutureLoop is an anonymous, verification-first research publication. Because we trade entirely on our verifiability rather than personal names, our corrections policy is transparent and permanent.

We do not execute quiet rewrites or silent deletions of posts that aged poorly. If we make an error in our numbers, metrics, or citations, we log the correction openly below with a date stamp, leaving the struck-through original claim visible.

Found an error? Send the details, including the primary source document, to: corrections@thefutureloop.com. Every verified error will be added to this log immediately.
