Our Methodology

The core standard of FutureLoop: We read the primary source and check whether the number holds up.

🔒 Why We Are Anonymous

We cover high-scale startups, active investment distributions, and regulatory affairs—topics that directly affect business valuations, capital raises, and corporate careers. A public byline introduces external pressure: access requests, public relations lobbying, and personal incentives. Pressure inevitably bends analysis.

By maintaining absolute anonymity, our incentive is simple: we have no personal brand to trade on, and no relationships to protect. Our credibility is carried entirely by our verifiability.

📡 Sourcing & Sifting

We monitor over 100 raw feeds, academic indexes, and engineering outputs daily. However, attention is not evidence. We ignore secondary press announcements and clickbait aggregators. Every item we select must lead directly to a primary dataset: peer-reviewed journals, verified preprints, open-source repositories, or official corporate engineering logs.

Monday: Compute & Semis

Silicon process nodes, packaging, memory bandwidth, interconnect bottlenecks, fab capex, and datacenter grid logistics.

Tuesday: AI Research

Model architectures, ablation results, loss curves, dataset leakages, and benchmark replication audits.

High-availability databases, P2P networking protocols, memory compilers, and production agent tools.

Clinical trial results, GCP protocols, drug safety, target discovery, and computational molecular screens.

Fusion energy milestones, solid-state batteries, aerospace alloys, and orbit launch systems.

Saturday: Weekly Signal

The weekly synthesis briefing featuring our deepest, most rigorous analytical deep-dives.

🪜 The Evidence Ladder

Every scientific claim or code performance metric we report is graded according to its validation tier. We separate pure computational speculation from physical wet-lab or production deployment results:

Tier Meaning Validation Type
E1 Hypothesis Only Theoretical design, preprint speculation, or non-peer-reviewed abstract.
E2 Computational Evidence In-silico model predictions, simulated datasets, or local benchmarks.
E3 Internal Experimental Validation In-house wet-lab assays, proprietary test suite evaluations, or closed-group pilots.
E4 Independent Replication Third-party replication, multi-center trials, or open-source peer reproductions.
E5 Production Deployment Live, large-scale consumer or industrial use in uncontrolled environments.

🛡️ Independence & Conflict Policy

We do not publish sponsored analysis, paid paper summaries, or developer promotional spots. If a tool or model is covered, it is because its codebase or benchmarks proved interesting under our audit.
